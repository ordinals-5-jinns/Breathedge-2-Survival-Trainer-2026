## 🎮 About the Game

Breathedge 2 is a humorous first-person survival adventure set in space. Developed by RedRuins Softworks and released in Early Access on August 31, 2026, it combines exploration, resource gathering, crafting, upgrades, repairs, combat, quests, base building, and open-space survival.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Breathedge 2**. This trainer simplifies survival and exploration by managing oxygen, temperature, resources, equipment condition, crafting materials, and combat encounters. It is designed for the Early Access version of Breathedge 2.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to focus on the story, exploration, crafting, quests, and unusual space adventures without managing every survival hazard or resource limitation.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the open-space environment carefully and monitor oxygen and temperature while traveling away from safe areas. Enable survival options before long expeditions so you can focus on reaching abandoned stations, wrecks, settlements, and optional locations.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect resources and useful junk, then use them to craft tools and equipment, learn blueprints, upgrade gear, and repair machinery. Resource and equipment options are most useful when preparing for major crafting projects or extended exploration trips.

### World & Environment

Breathedge 2 centers on story-driven exploration across a large space area filled with quests, NPCs, events, strange devices, shipwrecks, and hidden discoveries. Use the trainer to reduce survival pressure while searching inaccessible areas and advancing through the first major story chapter.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is part of the survival adventure, with hostile Coffinbots and other dangers threatening the player. Activate defensive options before encounters, then disable them when you want a more balanced challenge.
